Spageti Carbonara

Spageti Carbonara is a classic Italian pasta dish made with eggs, cheese, pancetta, and pepper. It's a simple yet delicious recipe that can be prepared in just a few minutes.

Spageti Carbonara made by combining cooked spaghetti with a creamy sauce made from eggs, cheese, and pancetta.

Ingredients

  • Spaghetti
  • Eggs
  • Cheese
  • Pancetta
  • Pepper

Instructions

  1. Cook the spaghetti according to the package instructions.
  2. In a separate pan, cook the pancetta until crispy.
  3. In a bowl, whisk together the eggs and cheese.
  4. Drain the spaghetti and return it to the pot.
  5. Add the pancetta and egg mixture to the spaghetti and toss to combine.
  6. Season with pepper to taste and serve immediately.

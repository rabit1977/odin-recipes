Ceasar Salad

Ceasar Salad is a classic salad made with romaine lettuce, croutons, parmesan cheese, and a creamy dressing. It's a simple yet delicious recipe that can be prepared in just a few minutes.

Ceasar Salad made by combining romaine lettuce with croutons, parmesan cheese, and a creamy dressing.

Ingredients

  • Romaine lettuce
  • Croutons
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Creamy dressing

Instructions

  1. Wash and chop the romaine lettuce.
  2. In a large bowl, combine the lettuce, croutons, and parmesan cheese.
  3. Add the creamy dressing and toss to combine.
  4. Serve immediately.
